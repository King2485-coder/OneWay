Payment privacy

Card details stay with authorized providers.

OneWay does not directly store complete payment card details. Payments are processed by authorized payment providers, which may securely retain payment credentials when permitted by the customer and required to complete purchases or subscriptions.

Marketplace clarity

Seller sales belong to the buyer and seller.

Independent sellers set their own return policies, subject to law and OneWay marketplace requirements. Processing fees, seller payouts, taxes, shipping, and any disclosed platform fee stay separate from OneWay subscription revenue.

FAQ

Clear answers, careful claims.

What can I use for free?

OneWay Free includes the OneWay ID, private personal messaging, OneWay-to-OneWay voice and video, Chirp, Communities, Quantum, basic contacts and Cloud access, buyer access to Shops, one basic Shop with 12 active listings, and one basic Site draft.

Does a core OneWay plan include a telephone number?

No. Core plans include OneWay-to-OneWay communication. A telephone number, calls to ordinary phone numbers, incoming ordinary phone calls, voicemail, caller ID, and supported SMS or MMS require a separate OneWay Phone service.

What does OneWay collect?

OneWay collects the minimum information reasonably required to provide and protect the service. Limited information may be required for account security, billing, seller verification, tax, shipping, fraud prevention, telecom, emergency-service, or legal obligations.

Does OneWay store complete card details?

OneWay does not directly store complete payment card details. Authorized payment providers process payments and may securely retain payment credentials when the customer permits it and the provider needs them for purchases or subscriptions.

Can I cancel or request a refund?

Subscriptions may be cancelled to prevent future renewal. Delivered or consumed digital services are generally final once used, but refunds may be issued when required by law, payment-platform policy, billing error, duplicate charge, or verified service failure. Independent Shop sellers set their own policies subject to law and marketplace requirements.

Can public prices change automatically?

No. The OneWay Pricing Agent may recommend changes, but an owner must approve them and the website, app, Stripe configuration, and legal copy must be updated together.
