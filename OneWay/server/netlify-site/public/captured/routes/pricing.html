Plain-language details

Understand every important limit.

PSTN

The ordinary public telephone network. OneWay-to-OneWay calls use OneWay communication and are separate from measured PSTN minutes.

Product slots

The maximum number of active product listings a seller can publish at one time.

Pooled storage

Storage shared across the members and resources in a business account.

Team members

People who can receive managed access to the business account and its shared tools.

Active Site

A published Site counted against the active plan limit. Draft limits may differ.

Active Shop

A Shop currently available to buyers and counted against the plan limit.

Fair use

Reasonable safeguards that protect platform performance and prevent abusive or automated overuse.

Marketplace fees

Any OneWay platform fee must be disclosed before checkout. Payment processors, shipping, tax, Ads, and third-party costs are separate.

OneWay-to-OneWay calls

Calls between people using OneWay. They do not include calls to or from ordinary telephone numbers.

Cancel future renewal

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled to prevent future renewal. Cancellation does not reverse capacity or usage already delivered.

OneWay service refunds

Activated capacity, consumed usage, completed AI generation, published services, and delivered one-time upgrades are generally final once used. Refunds may be issued when required by law, platform policy, billing error, duplicate charge, or verified service failure.

Seller merchandise refunds

Independent sellers set return, exchange, and refund policies subject to law and OneWay marketplace requirements. App Store refunds and payment-processor disputes follow their respective provider processes.
