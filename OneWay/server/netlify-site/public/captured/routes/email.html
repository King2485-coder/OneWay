Mailbox essentials

Useful email, without turning every open and click into a profile.

Real internet email

Send to and receive from ordinary email providers. Reply, reply all, forward, save drafts, search, and organize mail in one mailbox.

Protected storage

Message bodies and sensitive attachment metadata are encrypted at rest. Downloads require mailbox ownership and an authenticated OneWay session.

Tracking off by default

OneWay does not enable hidden open pixels or click tracking by default. Remote-image controls are part of the public launch gate.

Privacy reality

Internet email is not automatically end-to-end encrypted.

When you email Gmail, Outlook, or another outside provider, that provider’s mail servers must receive the message. OneWay protects its own storage and transport, but does not label ordinary external email as end-to-end encrypted.

Personal and business

One mailbox first. Team mailboxes later.

The first release centers on one personal @oneway.is mailbox. Additional aliases, custom domains, shared support or sales addresses, retention tools, and staff permissions will only be described as included after plan costs and access controls are approved.

Before public launch

Delivery and safety must prove themselves.

✓ MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC verification

✓ Gmail and Outlook round-trip tests

✓ Bounce and complaint processing

✓ Attachment malware scanning

✓ Spam and abuse controls

✓ Notification and deep-link tests

✓ Published privacy and terms language

✓ Provider costs and support approval

Until every gate passes, the app shows setup status and refuses live delivery instead of pretending a message was sent.
