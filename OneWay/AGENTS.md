# OneWay Project Operating Guidance

## Promotion Command Center

- Treat ongoing organic promotion as a standing product and operating requirement: OneWay should maintain an active online presence every day, with multiple promotion opportunities across the day.
- Do not reduce the Command Center to a campaign-draft screen. Product plans, implementation decisions, and recurring workflows must account for content planning, publishing readiness, execution status, and measurable follow-through.
- Use only official OneWay channels that the owner has connected or explicitly authorized. Never publish through personal accounts, expose credentials, bypass platform controls, or purchase ads without explicit owner approval.
- Keep promotional claims truthful and supported by the current product. Vary useful content rather than repeating identical posts, respect each platform's rules and frequency limits, and preserve an owner-review path for sensitive, legal, pricing, privacy, security, or paid-placement claims.
- When publishing is blocked by a missing connection or approval, prepare channel-ready material and report the exact blocker and safest owner action instead of treating the run as successful.

## Commercial Video Quality Standard

- Treat `Docs/Marketing/Commercials/ReferenceStandard/oneway-commercial-quality-reference.mp4` as the minimum visual, editorial, and audio quality benchmark for every OneWay commercial.
- Commercials must be footage-led cinematic stories with realistic motion, believable fictional people, natural device interaction, practical lighting, deliberate camera movement, coherent continuity, and a single clear use case. A slideshow, still-image montage, generic product deck, or slowly animated poster is not an acceptable commercial.
- Show authentic current OneWay UI clearly on the device or as a motivated close-up. Do not invent screens, unreadable interface text, unsupported capabilities, privacy guarantees, or product claims.
- Use concise pacing: establish the product immediately, show a person using it in context, show the resulting action or benefit, then close on a brief branded end card. Every shot must advance that story.
- Match the reference standard for sound: purposeful music and sound effects, clean transitions, social-ready loudness, and narration only when it materially improves the commercial. Do not cover weak visuals with excessive voiceover or captions.
- Reject and regenerate footage with uncanny anatomy, identity drift, inconsistent devices, floating objects, fake-looking UI, broken text, mismatched lighting, or visibly static image treatment.
- Review the finished export shot by shot and at normal playback before presenting it. Measure duration, resolution, frame rate, codec, and audio loudness; preserve a production ledger and the accepted source assets.
- Never publish a commercial automatically. Present the completed review master to the owner and wait for explicit approval before any upload, scheduling, or posting.
