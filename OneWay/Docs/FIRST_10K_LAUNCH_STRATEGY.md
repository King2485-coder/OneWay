# OneWay First 10K Launch Strategy

## Positioning

OneWay wins early by focusing on three groups that naturally create content and commerce loops:

- Creators on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts
- Small businesses that already sell through DMs and live sessions
- Gen Z social shoppers who respond to urgency, identity, and shareable discovery

The product promise should stay simple:

- private, premium live communication
- creator-led shopping
- fast checkout
- AI-powered selling assistance

## Phase 1: 0 to 1,000 Users

Primary goal:

- recruit 50 to 100 creators manually

Offer:

- 0 percent platform fees
- free promotion inside the app
- early access badge
- higher affiliate share during launch

Execution:

- DM creators with a one-line pitch and a custom invite link
- onboard each creator into a weekly launch cohort
- help each creator run at least one live session and publish one daily clip

Success metrics:

- 30 percent of invited creators activate
- 60 percent of activated creators publish content in 7 days
- each active creator invites at least 2 more users

## Phase 2: 1,000 to 5,000 Users

Primary goal:

- convert creator output into daily organic growth

Content loops:

- "watch me sell this live"
- "AI creator picks today's best products"
- "top products from tonight's live"

Product loops:

- trending lives row
- featured creator cards
- sponsored placements
- push moments built around social proof

Success metrics:

- 20 percent weekly retention
- 1.5 average invites per active user
- 1 live join per active user per week

## Phase 3: 5,000 to 10,000 Users

Primary goal:

- amplify urgency and social proof

Launch levers:

- trending leaderboard
- flash sale countdowns
- "500 people watching now" push notifications
- boosted placement for creators who bring buyers

Success metrics:

- 25 percent live-to-purchase conversion on top sessions
- 2 invites per creator cohort user
- repeat purchase behavior in 14 days

## Product Features Supporting Growth

Already aligned in the app:

- invite/share banner in the shop feed
- AI creator row
- trending lives row
- sponsored product injection
- Apple Pay first with external checkout fallback

Next implementation targets:

- referral credit ledger
- invite attribution on signup
- creator leaderboard backend
- lifecycle push campaigns for live reminders and purchase urgency

## Daily Operating Rhythm

- recruit 10 creators
- publish 3 launch clips
- schedule 2 trending lives
- review conversion and invite metrics
- boost one winning product and one winning creator

## First 30 Days Metrics

- 1,000 downloads
- 100 daily active users
- 10 active creators
- 1 to 5 sales per day
- call success rate above 90 percent on launch cohort devices
- day-1 retention tracked across invitees and buyers

## Required Analytics Events

Track these from launch week onward:

- `app_launch`
- `call_started`
- `call_failed`
- `call_ended`
- `store_viewed`
- `product_clicked`
- `checkout_tapped`
- `paywall_shown`

Use these to answer:

- which creators drive live joins
- which products earn clicks
- where call setup fails
- when first-day users return
