# OneWay Privacy Policy

Draft content for publication at `https://oneway.app/privacy`.

## Overview

OneWay provides private messaging and real-time voice and video calling. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## Information we collect

- Account information you provide, such as email address and profile details
- Contact information, only if you grant permission to access your contacts
- Call metadata, such as caller, callee, timestamps, duration, and delivery status
- Device and notification tokens needed to deliver incoming call notifications
- Optional uploaded content such as voicemail audio or profile media

## How we use information

- To authenticate your account
- To connect calls and deliver real-time communication features
- To show your contacts and help you find people you know
- To deliver incoming call notifications through Apple PushKit
- To maintain service reliability, security, and abuse prevention

## Calling and media handling

- Incoming call VoIP pushes are used only to notify the app about real incoming calls
- CallKit is used to present native incoming call controls on iPhone
- Audio and video are transmitted through our calling infrastructure to connect participants in real time
- Call metadata may be retained for call history, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, and reliability monitoring

## Encryption and security

- Signaling and account traffic are protected in transit using HTTPS and secure websocket connections
- OneWay uses session-level cryptographic protections during call setup
- We may continue improving the system with stronger end-to-end encryption upgrades over time

## Contacts

- Contacts are accessed only with your permission
- Contact data is used to help you discover and connect with people you know
- If contact sync is enabled, the app should process only the minimum data needed for matching and discovery

## Subscriptions and payments

- If subscriptions are offered to consumers inside the iOS app, payment must be processed using Apple's In-App Purchase system
- Business or enterprise billing may be handled outside the app where Apple's rules allow

## Data sharing

- We do not use VoIP push notifications for advertising or marketing
- We may share data with infrastructure providers that help us operate storage, notifications, networking, and security
- We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the safety and security of users and the service

## Your choices

- You can decline contact access
- You can stop using the service and request account deletion subject to operational or legal retention needs
- You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact: `privacy@oneway.app`
